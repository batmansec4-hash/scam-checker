Money sent by mistake? The wrong-transfer scam

You get a message that looks like a wallet notification, then a call: I sent you money by mistake, please send it back. The notification can be fake, but the money you send back is real.

Warning signs

  • A message about money received, followed quickly by a call asking you to return it.
  • Emotional pressure: the caller says they are desperate or in trouble.
  • A request to return the money to a different account than the one it came from.
  • The caller tells you not to contact official support.

What to do

  • Open your wallet or bank app and check your real balance. Do not trust an SMS or a screenshot.
  • If no money arrived, there is nothing to return.
  • If money really did arrive by mistake, ask your wallet or bank support to reverse it through their own process.
  • Block the number and keep screenshots as evidence.
