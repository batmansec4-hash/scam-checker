WhatsApp verification code requests: how accounts get hijacked

A friend or relative messages from a new number, or from a contact's real account, and asks for a code you just received. That code logs them in to your WhatsApp. They then message your contacts asking for money.

Warning signs

  • A request for the 6-digit code WhatsApp sent you.
  • A message from a new number claiming to be a friend.
  • An urgent money request from a contact who will not take a phone call.
  • A change in how the contact normally writes.

What to do

  • Never share a WhatsApp code with anyone.
  • Call the person on their usual number to check.
  • Turn on two-step verification in WhatsApp settings.
  • If you lose access, recover the account through WhatsApp's official process and warn your contacts by another channel.

Based on the National CERT of Pakistan advisory on WhatsApp account hijacking. Check official pages for current details.
