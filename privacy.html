Privacy

Messages you paste are checked inside your browser. They are not sent to our servers and they are not saved.

What we collect

Anonymous visit counts (pages viewed and country) through Vercel Web Analytics, which does not use cookies. We have no accounts and collect no names, numbers or message text.

Links

The checker looks at the text of a link but never opens it. Links to official complaint pages take you to other websites with their own privacy rules.

Ads

If we show ads in future, advertisers may use cookies, and we will update this page before that happens.
