Already shared a code or paid? What to do now

Stay calm and act quickly. Contacting your bank or wallet fast gives the best chance of limiting the damage. Pick what happened to you.

I shared an OTP, PIN or password

  • Call your wallet or bank now, using the number on your card or on its official website or app. JazzCash lists 4444 from a Jazz number.
  • Ask them to secure or block the account, and note the complaint number.
  • Change your PIN and passwords from your own phone, and anywhere else you used the same ones.

I sent money

  • Contact your bank or wallet at once and ask them to raise a dispute. Speed matters.
  • Save the receipt, transaction ID, account name and number, and screenshots of the chat.
  • Do not pay a recovery agent who promises to get your money back for a fee. That is a second scam.
  • Report it to NCCIA.

I clicked a link or installed an app

  • Turn on airplane mode and uninstall the app. Do not enter any more details.
  • From a different, trusted device, change the passwords for your bank, wallet, email and WhatsApp.
  • Tell your bank or wallet so they can watch for suspicious activity.

My WhatsApp was taken over

  • Reinstall WhatsApp and log in with your number to get your account back, then switch on two-step verification.
  • Tell friends and family by phone call that messages from your number asking for money are fake.
  • If you cannot get back in, use WhatsApp's official support.

Where to report

  • NCCIA complaint portal (the research lists helpline 1799)
  • PTA complaint system for telecom spam and spoofed numbers
  • State Bank Sunwai portal if your bank does not resolve a dispute
