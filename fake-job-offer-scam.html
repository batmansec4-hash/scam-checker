Fake job offers: fees, tasks and deposits

Messages offer easy online work with a high daily salary, or a job abroad. Then you are asked to pay a fee, or to deposit money to unlock your earnings. The money does not come back.

Warning signs

  • High pay for simple tasks like liking videos or reviewing products.
  • A registration, security or processing fee.
  • Small early payouts, followed by a request for a bigger deposit.
  • Recruiters who only use WhatsApp and have no company website.

What to do

  • Real employers do not charge you to hire you. Stop at the first fee.
  • Check the company through its official website and contact details.
  • Never send money to a personal wallet for a job.
  • Report the account on the platform where you found it.
