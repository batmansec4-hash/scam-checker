Parcel or customs fee links: courier scams in Pakistan

A text says your parcel is held, your address is incomplete, or a small customs fee is due. The link leads to a copy of a real website that collects your card or wallet details. Victims have reported losing far more than the small fee.

Warning signs

  • A parcel you were not expecting.
  • A small fee, a deadline, or a threat that the parcel will be returned or destroyed.
  • A shortened or unfamiliar link.
  • An APK or app download inside the message.

What to do

  • Do not open the link or install anything.
  • Check the delivery through the courier's official website or app, using the tracking number you were given.
  • Never enter card or wallet details on a page you reached from a text.
  • If you already paid, contact your bank or wallet immediately.

Based on a National CERT of Pakistan advisory on fake delivery messages and public reports. Check official pages for current details.
