About Scam Check PK

Scam Check PK is a free tool that checks SMS and WhatsApp messages against scam patterns reported in Pakistan. You paste a message, and it shows a verdict, the warning signs it found, and what to do next.

How it works

The check runs in your browser against patterns taken from public advisories by regulators, telecom companies and cybersecurity agencies. It does not open links in your message.

Limits

Scammers change their wording often, so the tool can miss new tricks. Treat the result as a guide, and verify through the official app or helpline.

Who built it

Built by a cybersecurity professional in Pakistan as a free public tool.
