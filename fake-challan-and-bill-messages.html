Fake e-challan and electricity bill messages

A text says you owe a traffic fine, or that your electricity will be cut in 30 minutes, with a link to pay. The page is fake and takes your card or wallet details.

Warning signs

  • A fine or bill you do not recognise.
  • A short deadline or a threat of disconnection.
  • A payment link from a personal mobile number.
  • A payment page that asks for a wallet PIN or OTP.

What to do

  • Check fines on the official traffic police or e-challan service yourself. Karachi Traffic Police has said genuine challan messages come from 8070.
  • Check your bill in your utility company's official app or on the paper bill.
  • Never pay through a link in a text.
  • Block the sender and report the message.

Based on Karachi Traffic Police warnings and news reports of utility scam alerts. Check official pages for current details.
