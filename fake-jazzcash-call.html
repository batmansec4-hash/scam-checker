Fake JazzCash or Easypaisa support call: what to do

A caller says they are from JazzCash or Easypaisa support. They tell you that you won a prize, that your wallet has a problem, or that your account will be blocked. Then they ask you to read out the OTP or PIN you just received. That code lets them take control of your wallet.

Warning signs

  • Someone asks for your OTP, PIN, password or a code sent to your phone.
  • A prize or reward you never applied for.
  • Pressure to act in minutes, or a threat to block your wallet.
  • The caller does not want you to hang up and call back.

What to do

  • Hang up. Never read out a code, even if the caller already knows your name.
  • JazzCash says on its official contact page that it never asks for your PIN, OTP or password.
  • Call the company on the number from its official website or app. JazzCash lists 4444 from a Jazz number.
  • If you shared a code, contact your wallet provider straight away and report it on the NCCIA complaint portal.

Based on advisories from Jazz and the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority. Check official pages for current details.
